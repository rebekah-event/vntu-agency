About

Rebekah Dennis

Event Director & Production Specialist

Rebekah Dennis is a freelance Event Director and Production Specialist with 9+ years of experience turning live event ideas into organised, professional and commercially useful experiences. She works with SMEs and enterprise organisations across B2B conferences, brand activations, leadership roundtables and community-led events — bringing the structure, supplier relationships and onsite leadership that busy teams need, without the overhead of a full agency.

Her approach is evidence-led: every event she produces comes with clear reporting on attendance, engagement, spend and outcomes, so clients can measure what happened and improve the next one. Rebekah has worked with organisations including the Financial Times, Informa Markets, TALiNT Partners and Investment Week, delivering everything from 4,500-person B2B conferences to intimate C-suite roundtables.
