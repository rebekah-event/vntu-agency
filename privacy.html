VNTU Agency is a trading name of VNTU Ltd, a UK-based event production company. Our contact details are: rebekah@vntu-events.com / 07572 680442 / London, United Kingdom.

We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

2. What Data We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect: your name, email address, company name (optional), event type (optional), and the content of your message. We do not collect payment information, sensitive personal data, or data from children.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to respond to your enquiry and, where you have indicated interest, to provide information about our services. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis

Our legal basis for processing your data is legitimate interest (responding to a direct enquiry you initiated). Where you subscribe to our mailing list, the basis is consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Third-Party Processors

Your contact form submissions are processed by Formspree (formspree.io), which stores submissions on servers in the United States under standard contractual clauses. This website is hosted by Cloudflare Pages and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 24 months or until the business relationship ends, whichever is sooner. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk). To exercise any right, email us at rebekah@vntu-events.com.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. Google Fonts may store a cookie for font delivery optimisation. You can block this via your browser settings without affecting site functionality.

We may update this policy periodically. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
